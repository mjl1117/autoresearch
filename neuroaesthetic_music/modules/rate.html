The Valence/Arousal Model

This task uses Russell's (1980) Circumplex Model of Affect to measure emotional response. Valence is the hedonic quality of a sound — how pleasant or unpleasant it feels (x-axis, left = negative, right = positive). Arousal is its activation level — how calm or energized it makes you feel (y-axis, bottom = calm, top = activated). Together they place any emotional response in a continuous two-dimensional space.
